# Destination

Meghalaya

# 1. Overview

- **Short introduction**: Meghalaya, often called the "Abode of Clouds," is a picturesque state in Northeast India known for its rolling hills, pristine forests, numerous waterfalls, deep caves, and unique living root bridges. It is blessed with abundant rainfall, contributing to its lush greenery and vibrant biodiversity.
- **Geography**: Meghalaya is a hilly state primarily comprising the Garo, Khasi, and Jaintia Hills. It shares its northern and eastern borders with Assam and its southern border with Bangladesh. The landscape is characterized by high plateaus, deep gorges, numerous rivers, and vast cave systems. The Khasi Hills region, particularly Cherrapunji and Mawsynram, holds the record for some of the highest rainfall in the world.
- **Climate**: The climate in Meghalaya is subtropical, with distinct monsoon, winter, and summer seasons. The state experiences heavy rainfall during the monsoon months (June to September). Winters are cool and dry, while summers are mild. The high altitude moderates temperatures, making it pleasant for most of the year, except for the peak monsoon's intense rain.
- **Why people visit**: People visit Meghalaya for its stunning natural beauty, including magnificent waterfalls (Nohkalikai, Seven Sisters), unique living root bridges (Double Decker Living Root Bridge), crystal-clear rivers (Dawki), and ancient cave systems (Mawjymbuin Cave, Mawsmai Cave). It offers opportunities for adventure tourism like trekking, caving, and river activities. The distinct indigenous cultures of the Khasi, Garo, and Jaintia tribes and their vibrant festivals also attract tourists seeking cultural experiences. The serene environment and pleasant climate offer a refreshing escape from urban life.
- **Interesting facts**:
    - Meghalaya is home to Mawsynram, which receives the highest average annual rainfall in the world.
    - The state is famous for its unique living root bridges, ancient wonders created by the Khasi people by training the roots of Ficus elastica trees.
    - It is one of the few matriarchal societies in the world, where lineage and inheritance are traced through the mother's side.
    - The state boasts some of the longest and deepest cave systems in India, attracting speleologists from around the globe.
    - The crystal-clear Umngot River in Dawki, where boats appear to float on glass, is a major attraction.

# 2. Quick Facts

- **State / Country**: Meghalaya / India
- **Time Zone**: Indian Standard Time (IST), UTC+5:30
- **Languages**: Khasi, Garo, Jaintia (Pnar), English (official), Hindi, Bengali, Assamese.
- **Currency**: Indian Rupees (INR)
- **Emergency Numbers**:
    - Police: 100
    - Fire: 101
    - Ambulance: 102
    - Disaster Management: 1070
    - Tourist Helpline: 1363 (National Toll-Free)
- **Airports**: Shillong Airport (Umroi Airport) (SHL) is the main airport. Lokpriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport (GAU) in Guwahati, Assam, is the closest major international airport, approximately 120 km from Shillong.
- **Railway Stations**: Meghalaya does not have an extensive railway network within the state. The nearest major railway station is Guwahati Railway Station (GHY) in Assam, which is well-connected to major Indian cities. Mendipathar is a small station within Meghalaya, but Guwahati is the primary railhead for visitors.
- **Typical Trip Duration**: A typical trip duration to Meghalaya ranges from 5 to 7 days to cover key attractions. A shorter trip of 3-4 days can cover Shillong and Cherrapunji, while a longer trip of 8-10 days allows for deeper exploration, including the Garo Hills and more remote areas.
- **Internet Connectivity**: Internet connectivity is generally good in major towns like Shillong and Cherrapunji, with 4G services available. Connectivity can be intermittent or weak in remote areas and some villages. Wi-Fi is available in most hotels and guesthouses.
- **Mobile Networks**: Major Indian mobile network providers like Jio, Airtel, and Vodafone Idea have coverage in Meghalaya. Signal strength is strong in urban centers but may be patchy or unavailable in very remote or hilly regions. It is advisable to carry a SIM card from a major provider.

# 3. Best Time to Visit

## Spring (March - April)

- **Weather**: Pleasant, mild, and relatively dry before the monsoon fully sets in. Clear skies are common.
- **Temperature**: Ranges from 15°C to 25°C.
- **Crowd levels**: Moderate. It's a popular time but less crowded than peak winter.
- **Pros**: Ideal weather for sightseeing and outdoor activities. Lush greenery from pre-monsoon showers. Waterfalls begin to fill up. Excellent for trekking and exploring.
- **Cons**: Can get a little warm towards late April. Some areas might start experiencing occasional pre-monsoon showers.
- **Festivals**: Shad Suk Mynsiem (Khasi dance festival in April).
- **Activities**: Trekking, caving, visiting living root bridges, boating in Dawki, exploring Shillong and Cherrapunji.
- **Budget impact**: Moderate. Accommodation and activity prices are standard.
- **Who should visit**: Ideal for trekkers, nature lovers, photographers, and those who prefer pleasant weather without extreme cold or heavy rain.
